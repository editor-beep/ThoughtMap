# Node Types

Each node type carries a distinct symbolic charge and suggests future visual and behavioral affordances.

### Thought
**Description**: A raw spark of cognition, insight, or reflection.  
**Emotional Meaning**: Clarity, emergence, quiet revelation.  
**Visual Behavior**: Soft cyan or white glow. Gentle pulsing when new. Simple geometric form that can evolve into more complex shapes.

### Joke
**Description**: A moment of humor, absurdity, or playful inversion.  
**Emotional Meaning**: Levity, surprise, cognitive release.  
**Visual Behavior**: Bright, erratic energy. Subtle animation or tilt. May leave faint trails when moved.

### Character
**Description**: A persona, archetype, fictional being, or real person rendered symbolically.  
**Emotional Meaning**: Presence, narrative potential, relational depth.  
**Visual Behavior**: Portrait-like silhouette or emblem. Distinct border styling. Can "face" other nodes.

### Myth
**Description**: Enduring stories, patterns, or archetypal narratives.  
**Emotional Meaning**: Timelessness, resonance across scales.  
**Visual Behavior**: Ornate, slightly ancient aesthetic. Faint ethereal particles or runic accents.

### Research
**Description**: Empirical findings, citations, or investigative threads.  
**Emotional Meaning**: Rigor, curiosity, pursuit.  
**Visual Behavior**: Clean, grid-like internal structure. Subtle measurement lines or coordinate accents.

### Canon
**Description**: Established truth, core belief, or foundational element within a domain.  
**Emotional Meaning**: Solidity, authority, anchor point.  
**Visual Behavior**: Stronger base, heavier visual weight, possible subtle pedestal or anchoring effect.

### Contradiction
**Description**: Tension, paradox, or opposing truths held simultaneously.  
**Emotional Meaning**: Friction, depth, intellectual honesty.  
**Visual Behavior**: Split coloring, subtle crack/glow effects, vibrating when nearby nodes create resonance.

### Artifact
**Description**: A created object, tool, artwork, or symbolic creation.  
**Emotional Meaning**: Craft, invention, tangibility.  
**Visual Behavior**: Object-like rendering. Possible 3D-ish tilt or material texture simulation.

### Fragment
**Description**: Incomplete, ephemeral, or broken pieces of thought.  
**Emotional Meaning**: Mystery, potential, humility.  
**Visual Behavior**: Translucent or shattered appearance. Connects loosely. May "heal" or evolve over time.

---

Future node types may be added, but all must carry clear emotional and symbolic weight.
